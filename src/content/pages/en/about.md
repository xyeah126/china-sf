This archive is a chronological and author-indexed illustrated record of Chinese science fiction and the literature of imagination, spanning antiquity to the present and linked to film and television works.

## Three tiers

To keep the record honest, every entry is assigned one of three tiers, shown as a badge on each card:

- **Myth**: supernatural beings, marvellous machines and distant lands, from antiquity to the Wei–Jin period.
- **Proto-SF**: machinery, flight and utopian societies from the Song to the mid-Qing, without a modern scientific framework.
- **Science fiction**: from the Late Qing onward, imagination within a modern scientific framework.

Works of antiquity are not science fiction; they are its *sources*. That distinction is this archive's scholarly baseline.

## On the origin of Chinese SF

- In 1903 Lu Xun introduced the concept of "science fiction" in his preface to the translation of De la Terre à la Lune.
- Moon Colony Novel (1904) is recognised as the first original Chinese science fiction novel.
- Before the Late Qing there was no science fiction as a genre — only seeds of scientific imagination.

## Inclusion criteria

A work is included when it meets at least two of the following: a Chinese author or first publication in Chinese; scientific, technological or futuristic content; a verifiable publication record; and recognised significance. Every entry cites its sources.

## Image policy: honesty first

Every image on the site is labelled with one of four provenance levels, shown as a badge on the card. The current distribution across 84 works:

- **Public domain (16)**: facsimiles of classical editions, late-Qing / early-Republic lithographs, Ming–Qing woodblock prints. Each carries a `coverSource` field naming the book, edition and scanning source for verification.
- **AI-generated illustrations (68)**: contemporary book covers, where no public-domain imagery exists. Every AI image is recorded as a *schematic illustration*, not a reproduction of the real cover; the prompt is preserved (`coverPrompt` field) so it can be re-rendered or replaced.
- **Licensed: none**. The archive holds no licensing agreements with any publisher, author or image library. If formal licenses are obtained in the future, the real cover will replace the placeholder with a clear credit.
- **Placeholder**: only used for film / TV posters (35 entries) and author portraits (31 entries) — see the dedicated sections below.

## Author portraits: seals, not photos

Copyright in a photograph belongs to the photographer, not the subject. The 31 authors range from long-deceased historical figures to living contemporary writers, and a living person's portrait is, in most jurisdictions, protected by both copyright and personality rights. **No author photos are hosted on this site**. Every name slot is rendered as a vermilion seal — a vertical Chinese name in seal script, with a small starfield derived from a hash of the name. Visually consistent, and zero legal exposure.

The seal font is `/fonts/seal-zhuan-subset.woff2`, a 38 KB subset of the MIT-licensed typeface *Yu Fan Xin Yu*. Chinese and English pages both use the Chinese name, so no Latin initials appear on the English side.

## Film & TV posters: copyright and the workaround

A film or TV poster is copyright of the producer or distributor. **Posters do not enter the public domain**, and fair use / fair dealing generally does not cover them: a poster is not the kind of text-based material that news reporting or academic citation exists to protect. The site therefore **does not scrape, mirror, or hot-link any image from Douban, IMDb, TMDB, or any other third-party platform**.

Current substitute (used for all 35 entries):

- The poster slot is a **type-coloured placeholder with a type abbreviation**, with a "Poster pending" tag in the corner.
- Each card carries **Douban and IMDb external links** pointing at the public search page of the other site — a hyperlink is not infringement, and lets the reader see the real poster where they already have an account.
- Where a Douban rating has been snapshotted, it is shown as plain text (`doubanRating` + `doubanRatingAt` with the capture date).

If a formal license is later obtained from the rights holder, the placeholder will be replaced and a credit line appended. Public-domain or CC-licensed posters are welcome via the issue tracker.

## Languages and translation

The site is bilingual. The language follows the visitor's system setting (unless the visitor has already made a manual choice on this site). Where an English entry is not yet written, the Chinese original is shown with a clear notice — **never a machine translation**. Fake English is a disservice to both English and Chinese readers; better to be absent.

## Corrections

Uncertain fields are marked on the page. Corrections are welcome via the repository's issue tracker. A reference site whose data cannot be trusted has no value: better to list less than to list wrong.
