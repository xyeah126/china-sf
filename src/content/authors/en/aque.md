A leading voice of China's "post-90s" science-fiction wave, A Que (pen name of Li Wei, b. 1990, Jingzhou, Hubei; graduated from Sichuan University) first published in 2011. His work — most famously the "Robot Series" collected in Living with Robots (2015, China's Cornerstone SF Library) and re-released as a full-length novel in 2021 (Living with Robots, Aviation Industry Press) — pairs cutting-edge technology with the ordinary hopes and doubts of young Chinese, treating AI ethics and coming-of-age as one subject. Gentle, romantic and unsparing, he is regarded as one of the most influential writers of the generation that followed the "new generation". Three-time Galaxy Award winner.
