A representative writer of the newer generation of Chinese science fiction and an electronics engineer from Sichuan, now based in Chengdu. He made his debut in 2004 with "Retrospection" and won the Galaxy Award for Best New Writer in 2005 for "Deep Impact." His subjects lean toward astronomy, computing and bioengineering; he calls himself a deliberately "hard" writer — stories grow out of the science, not the other way around. His novel Crossing Saturn's Rings (2020) won the Galaxy Award for Best Novel and the Xingyun Award gold prize for best novel in 2021. His other books include the collection The High-Frequency Trader and the novel A Child in the Cosmic Ripples.
