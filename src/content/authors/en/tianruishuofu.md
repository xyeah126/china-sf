An online science fiction writer under the China Literature platform, born in 1996 in Jiujiang, Jiangxi, with a biology degree from a Harbin university. He is known for hard-core premises wedded to localised, vernacular storytelling — his stated method is to bind classic SF concepts to specifically Chinese settings and life. His novel Dead on Mars won the Galaxy Award for best online literature; We Live in Nanjing took the Galaxy Award for best online science fiction and the 2022 Xingyun Award gold prize for best novel, was named an "Outstanding Work" of the national Five-One Project, and was acquired by the British Library; Silence of Titan won the Galaxy Award for best original book.
