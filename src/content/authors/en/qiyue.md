A Chinese science fiction writer, real name Zhao Lei, a native of Sichuan with a graduate degree in biology from Nanjing University. He debuted in 2001 with "The Tianhuo Incident," then spent a decade in the games industry before returning to Chengdu in 2016 and to writing. His comeback novel The Stars (2019) embeds hard science fiction in the texture of Chengdu — a FAST signal, an anti-terror operation and the mortgage anxieties of young scientists woven into one plot — and won the gold prize for best novel at the 11th Xingyun Awards, topped Douban's 2019 original science fiction list, and was announced for a Tencent series adaptation. His 2021 novel The Strange Tales of a Small Town sets cosmic reverie against the Third Front construction memory of his hometown Hanwang, extending what he calls science fiction with distinctly Chinese feeling.
