A contemporary Chinese SF writer and journalist, vice-chair of the Chinese Writers' Association Science-Fiction Committee. With Liu Cixin, Wang Jinkang and He Xi he is counted among the "Four Heavenly Kings" of modern Chinese science fiction. His dystopian, Kafkaesque fiction uses subway cars, high-speed trains and hospitals as the visible skin of an invisible universe of political control and mass hallucination; longer works include the Subway/High-Speed Rail/Hospital trilogy, Mars Shines on America, The Red Sea and Exorcism. Multiple Galaxy Award, Xingyun Award and World Chinese SF Art Award winner.
