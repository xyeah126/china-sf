Living with Robots (novel edition) is a full-length SF novel by A Que, published by Aviation Industry Press in 2021. Set in the 2070s, when intelligent robots have saturated Chinese service, military and domestic life and almost all of them are produced by a single company — Territory Corp. — the protagonist Chen Zechuan, just released from a six-month prison sentence for a chance legal infringement, is assigned a domestic companion robot named LW31 to evaluate his behaviour for the next half year. The strict yet clumsy, tirelessly loyal LW31 draws Chen into a strange and unsettling live experiment that turns both their lives around.

The novel continues A Que's "Robot Series" — begun in his 2013 short fiction and gathered in his 2015 collection of the same title (Living with Robots, Sichuan Science and Technology Press) — and re-imagines it in long-form: the question of what it means for a machine to share a life with a human is taken out of the lab and into the texture of family, class and quiet Chinese power. Written with A Que's characteristic gentleness, restraint and melancholy, it grounds technological loneliness in the most ordinary feelings of the body. Filed as SF.
