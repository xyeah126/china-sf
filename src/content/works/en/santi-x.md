A work of fan fiction based on the Three-Body trilogy, serialised online by Baoshu in 2010 and later published in book form. It is among the best-known and most discussed pieces of Chinese science fiction fan writing.

Through the perspective of a contemplator, it retells and extends the cosmic history of the trilogy, adding dimensions, forms of civilisation and ultimate questions that the original left undeveloped.

Its imagination lies in the further development of an existing premise: working inside Liu Cixin's framework of cosmic sociology, it continues to extrapolate the evolution of civilisations and the end of the universe, in a relation to the original that is at once responsive and competitive.

It provoked continuing debate on the boundary between fan writing and original work, and brought Baoshu into science fiction. Filed as SF.

The legitimacy of fan fiction has been at the centre of the debate around this work. Its defenders hold that it extends ground the original left undeveloped; its critics that it dissolves the original's openness. Either way it demonstrates something: when an invented world is sufficiently self-consistent and explanatory, readers will enter it and go on extrapolating for themselves — perhaps the most practical measure of whether a science-fictional premise has succeeded.
