A novel by Zhang Ran published around 2015, set against an anomaly of the sun and following humanity's response to the crisis.

Solar activity turns abnormal and the Earth faces catastrophe. Within a narrowing window, nations must cooperate and compete by extreme means, and space becomes the final arena.

Its imagination continues the sustained Chinese interest in the sun as a subject, but shifts the emphasis from flight to political contest under crisis. The pacing is tight and the depiction of international politics concrete.

Joining grand catastrophe to individual fates, it balances readability with speculation. Filed as SF.

Compared with the solar crisis of The Wandering Earth, this novel is more down to earth: rather than imagining the removal of the whole planet, it has nations competing for a chance to save themselves within a narrow window. Zhang thereby writes space as an extension of geopolitics rather than an exit from Earth. The orientation shows a shift of interest among younger Chinese SF writers, from grand technological solutions to the extrapolation of realpolitik.

This is one of the clearest divisions between the newer generation of Chinese SF writers and their predecessors.
