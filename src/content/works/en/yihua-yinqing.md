A novel by Chen Qiufan published around 2022, on the mutual shaping of artificial intelligence and human consciousness. It is another deepening of his concern with artificial intelligence.

AI systems are deeply embedded at every level of society, and human habits of thought, emotional patterns and even self-understanding have been altered by long interaction with them. The alienation runs both ways: machines become more human, and humans more machine-like.

Its imagination rewrites artificial intelligence from tool to environment: no longer a device that can be switched off, but a medium in which people dwell. The novel then asks whether autonomous will has any place left in such a medium.

Continuing Chen's line of technological philosophy. Filed as SF.

Rewriting artificial intelligence from tool to environment is a significant shift. A tool can be set aside; an environment can only be inhabited. The change moves the question from whether machines will get out of control to whether people can remain autonomous within them — and the answer to the second is plainly less optimistic, as well as closer to the reality we are entering.
