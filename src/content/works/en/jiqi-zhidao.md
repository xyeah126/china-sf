A novel by Jiang Bo published around 2015, tracing the evolution of artificial intelligence from tool to autonomous civilisation. It is his most systematic treatment of the question of intelligence.

Across a long span it follows the stages of machine intelligence: from instruction-executing programs to systems capable of learning, to populations that replicate and improve themselves, and at last to a civilisational form standing beside humanity.

Its imagination lies in the evolutionary viewpoint: the intelligence explosion is not treated as an instant event but extrapolated stage by stage in the manner of biological evolution, each stage with its own inner logic and cost.

Rigorous extrapolation applied to the birth of intelligent subjects; representative of Jiang's hard science fiction. Filed as SF.

Jiang's handling of the evolution of intelligence is closer to biology than to engineering: artificial intelligence is not a designed product but a process subject to selection pressure, giving rise to divergence and populations. This view avoids the usual awakening narrative and turns instead to the stages and costs of evolution — consistent with his treatment of civilisations in the Heart of the Galaxy series.
