The concluding volume of the trilogy, published in 2016, in which the riddle of civilisational survival is answered at the heart of the galaxy.

Reaching the galactic centre at last, the protagonist confronts not only the true face of the enemy but the fundamental questions of the cycle of galactic civilisations and of his own place in it. The several threads are gathered here, and a complete cosmology is offered.

Its imagination reaches the series' maximum in this final volume: black hole, time, the recurrence of civilisations and individual choice are placed in a single scene, while Jiang keeps credibility at the technical level.

Closing on a complete cosmology, the series is a significant achievement in Chinese space opera. Filed as SF.

The difficulty of a finale is the gathering up: three volumes have laid out the threads of space, time and the cycle of civilisations, and any careless resolution would void the weight of what came before. Jiang's answer is to write the solution as a leap of perspective — arriving at the heart of the galaxy, the protagonist confronts no former enemy but the mechanism of the cycle itself. The series thus ends complete and yet not closed.

It is also the most lasting impression the series leaves with its readers.
