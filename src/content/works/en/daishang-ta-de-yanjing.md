A celebrated short story by Liu Cixin published in 1999, long anthologised and among his most widely circulated short works.

The narrator puts on a pair of sensor goggles that let someone who cannot leave see the world through his senses. He takes those eyes to the grassland, to flowers, to the wind. Only at the end is it revealed that she is an astronaut trapped at the Earth's core, unable ever to return to the surface.

Its imagination works on two levels: the device, which turns sensory experience into transmissible data, and the extreme situation at the Earth's core, which pushes loneliness to a physical limit.

Enormous loneliness rendered from a minimal premise; among the most moving short stories in Chinese science fiction. Filed as SF.

The story is often placed among Liu Cixin's tender pieces, together with The Rural Teacher. Almost all its force comes from the final revelation: an apparently ordinary journey is redefined at the last moment as a farewell. Liu accomplishes the reversal of perspective within a very short span, without a word of explanation, showing his exact control of narrative pace.
