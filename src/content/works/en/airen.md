The Cancerian is a full-length novel by Wang Jinkang, published in 2003 by Henan People's Publishing House as part of the "New-Human" trilogy. Without his knowledge, a biologist named Weiss uses cells from his late grandmother — HeLa cells, the famous line of immortal cancer cells — to clone a Black girl with extraordinary growth and regenerative powers. From the moment of her birth, she is hunted by an organ-trafficking ring and a self-styled "League for the Preservation of Human Purity".

The novel asks, with quiet engineering anger, who has the right to decide what counts as a human being in a technological society. Wang writes the African-American girl and the HeLa-derived body as one continuous figure: the cancer gene proves more durable than the human whose cells it grew in, and the book turns that biological fact into a stark parable of race, capital and survival. The Cancerian sits at the heart of Wang Jinkang's four-part "New Human" cycle alongside The Humanoid, The Leopards and The Sea-Men. Filed as SF.
