He Xi's signature work, published in 2002 and winner of the Chinese Science Fiction Galaxy Award. Using the Buddhist six paths as a figure, it conceives the multiple structure of the universe and the condition of the beings within it.

The universe is not one continuous whole but several incommensurable strata; beings in each stratum obey their own physical laws, yet the strata intersect through some mechanism. Investigating anomalies, the protagonist approaches the truth of this structure.

Its imagination joins the cosmological diagram of Eastern philosophy to the contemporary hypothesis of a multiverse. The six paths cease to be a religious notion and become an extrapolable cosmological model.

Rare metaphysical writing in Chinese science fiction, and among He Xi's most important works. Filed as SF.

The choice of the six paths shows He Xi's method with cosmic material: rather than ready-made scientific vocabulary, he borrows an indigenous conceptual scheme already elaborated over centuries and fills it with extrapolable content. The advantage is that readers already have an intuition for it; the risk is that it looks mystical. He Xi's answer is to write each path as a verifiable physical condition, grounding the metaphor.
