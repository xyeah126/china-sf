The Leopards is a full-length novel by Wang Jinkang, published in 2003 by Henan People's Publishing House as part of the "New-Human" trilogy. Xie Baofei, a young Chinese-American, smashes the world sprint record — then cannot stop replaying the four-year-old tragedy that made him who he is. The truth has been hidden inside his own family for decades.

The novel puts athletic ambition and genetic engineering on the same operating table. If an ordinary runner is given a leopard's speed and explosive power by surgery, what separates him from his family — and from being human? Wang uses the sprint as a figure of the question, and answers it in a domain that holds the body at its most exposed: love is the last and only ground that the engineered body cannot outrun. The Leopards is the second panel of Wang Jinkang's four-novel "New Human" cycle alongside The Humanoid, The Cancerian and The Sea-Men. Filed as SF.
