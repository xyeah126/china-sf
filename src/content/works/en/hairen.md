The Sea-Men is a full-length novel by Wang Jinkang, published in 2003 by Henan People's Publishing House as the third panel of the "New-Human" trilogy. Submarine captain Rams wakes from a 270-year cryogenic sleep to find that an intelligent sea-people — dolphin-men — has taken the world; the human descendants he himself bred have dwindled, and both species treat him as a kind of god.

The novel uses a centuries-long sleep and a first contact between species as a way of pressing the same question: if humans are no longer the planet's only intelligent life, what holds old memories and new myths together? Wang Jinkang sets love, legend and cruelty on the same tide — an elegiac song about a species that has just discovered it is no longer alone. The Sea-Men closes the four-novel "New Human" cycle that began with The Humanoid. Filed as SF.
