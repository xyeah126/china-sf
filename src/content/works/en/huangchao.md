A novel by Chen Qiufan published in 2013, a landmark of the Chinese science fiction new wave. It turns the methods of cyberpunk to questions of environmental justice and globalisation.

The story is set on Silicon Isle, a fictional southern island that receives the world's electronic waste. Foreign capital, local clans, migrant workers and the waste-processing industry are entangled there. Mimi, a waste worker despised by the locals, undergoes an inexplicable change of consciousness after an accident.

Its imagination works on two levels: the cyberpunk apparatus of body modification and consciousness upload, and the depiction of the e-waste industry as a complete globalised space. Their conjunction gives technological anxiety a concrete material basis.

Seen as a milestone of the Chinese SF new wave, it has appeared in several translations. Filed as SF.

The choice of electronic waste is the novel's surest stroke: it makes globalisation not an abstraction but an island one can smell. Chen transplants the customary neon and prosthetics of cyberpunk to a southern island of scrap and cheap labour, giving the mode a material basis of its own in a Chinese setting.
