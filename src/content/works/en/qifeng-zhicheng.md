A representative short story by Zhang Ran published around 2013, on shifting relations between wind, city and people. It is among his most popular works.

The story is set in a perennially windy city where wind is both a climatic phenomenon and the fundamental force shaping urban form and ways of life. The characters' memories and choices in the wind carry the emotional thread.

Its imagination is of the environmental-premise kind: a dominant natural condition serves as the fulcrum from which architecture, transport, custom and emotional expression are extrapolated. Zhang's touch is delicate and avoids reliance on spectacle.

It represents the more literary strand of Chinese science fiction. Filed as SF.

At its heart the story is an environmental one: when a natural condition governs life in a place over a long time, the relation to it ceases to be mere adaptation and becomes a vessel for feeling and memory. Zhang writes wind as both climate and fate, so that the choices his characters make carry a sense of destiny. This method of writing people through their environment gives the story a distinctly literary texture beyond its science fiction.
