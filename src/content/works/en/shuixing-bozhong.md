A short story by Wang Jinkang published around 2002, on the idea of cultivating life on Mercury, a planet of extremes. Brief but weighty, it is among his finest short works.

A team on Mercury, close to the sun, sows and sustains the first forms of life by special means. The task runs over generations, and its participants must accept that they will never see the outcome.

Its imagination works on two levels: a technical scheme for maintaining life in extreme conditions, and a sacrifice on the scale of time — handing an undertaking to a future one will not witness, which lends emotional weight to the hard premise.

It represents Wang's manner of cool technique and warm treatment: rigorous in premise, landing on human choice and transmission. Filed as SF.

The story has a particular structure: its narrative spans several generations, each seeing only a short stretch of the task. Wang thereby makes transmission a component of hard science fiction — technique itself generates no meaning; the organisation and conviction that carry it forward do. This gives an austere space premise an emotional weight comparable to that of The Heartbroken.
