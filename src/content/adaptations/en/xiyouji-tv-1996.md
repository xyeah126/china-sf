Produced by Hong Kong's TVB in 1996 and directed by Lau Sai-yu, starring Dicky Cheung.
Working in Cantonese, the series adds a great deal of comedy and departs markedly from the novel's tone.
Cheung's lively, irreverent Monkey became a shared memory for a generation of Hong Kong viewers.
Changes in casting and rights later handed the lead to Benny Chan in its sequel.
The version shows how a classic text can be reinterpreted across regional cultures.
