Directed by Cheang Pou-soi and released in 2014, starring Donnie Yen, Chow Yun-fat and Aaron Kwok.
It concentrates on the havoc in heaven, with action as its chief selling point.
Yen, who also designed the action, makes the Monkey King's movements sharp and fast.
Its 3D and effects spending was first-rank for a Chinese blockbuster at the time.
Reviewers praised the action but found the storytelling comparatively thin.
