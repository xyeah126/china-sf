The Adventures of Super Family, directed by Song Yang and released in 2023, stars Allen and Shen Teng.
A family unexpectedly acquires superpowers, and their life changes accordingly.
The superpower premise serves comic conflict rather than any extrapolation of its social consequences.
The film continues the comic style of Mahua FunAge; the SF and fantasy elements serve family material and gags.
It is among the recent representatives of soft superpower subjects in Chinese-language cinema.
