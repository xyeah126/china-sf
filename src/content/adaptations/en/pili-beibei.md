Thunderbolt Baby, directed by Song Chong and released in 1988, is a children's science fiction film and a classic of Chinese SF cinema.
It tells of a boy, Beibei, born carrying an electric charge, whose difference brings both adventures and trouble.
The SF premise is joined to a child's growing up: the charge is at once a marvel and a figure for the loneliness of being unlike others.
Chinese children's science fiction of the 1980s was scarce, and this film's full conception and sincere feeling made it a shared memory for a generation.
It is often paired with Death Ray on a Coral Island as a representative work of early Chinese science fiction cinema.
