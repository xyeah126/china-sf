Boonie Bears: Back to Earth, directed by Lin Huida and released in 2022, belongs to the Boonie Bears series.
It transposes the series' customary comic adventure to a setting of space and alien civilisation.
Bramble acquires alien powers by accident and is drawn into a crisis touching the whole Earth.
As an animation for children, it serves to introduce space imagining to a very young audience.
The series is a rare instance in Chinese animation of sustained reliance on science fiction subjects.
