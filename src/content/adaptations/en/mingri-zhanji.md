Warriors of Future, directed by Ng Yuen-fai and released in 2022, is a Chinese-language mecha science fiction film long championed by its star Louis Koo.
Sean Lau, Carina Lau and Nick Cheung co-star; the story is set in a future where the environment has degraded and cities survive under domes.
A squad must complete its mission within a very short window to save an entire city threatened by contamination.
Its significance is chiefly industrial: a rare attempt in Chinese-language cinema at mecha and heavy-industrial design, years in preparation.
Its narrative is comparatively thin, but as a case in the industrialisation of Chinese science fiction film it remains worth attention.
