The 1986 CCTV adaptation, directed by Yang Jie, took six years to film.
Liu Xiao Ling Tong plays Sun Wukong; Xu Shaohua and Chi Chongrui successively play Xuanzang, with Ma Dehua as Zhu Bajie and Yan Huaili as Sha Wujing.
Across twenty-five episodes it stages the pilgrimage and remains the most widely circulated screen version of the novel.
Limited by the technology of its day, its effects were achieved with practical tricks and models, producing a distinctive homespun aesthetic.
Rebroadcast for decades, it is among the most rerun productions in Chinese television history.
