CJ7, written and directed by and starring Stephen Chow, released in 2008, brings alien life into a warm comedy.
Xu Jiao plays Dicky Chow; the film tells of an impoverished father and son and a creature from space called CJ7.
The science fictional element does not build a world so much as catalyse feeling: the marvel CJ7 brings changes the father's and son's circumstances.
The work continues Chow's customary view from below and his tragicomic method; science fiction is only the shell.
It is among the most natural couplings of SF elements with family drama in Chinese-language cinema.
