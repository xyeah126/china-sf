The Disappearance of the Atmosphere, directed by Feng Xiaoning and released in 1990, is a rare Chinese science fiction film on an environmental subject.
The Earth's atmosphere has developed a hole through leaked chemicals; ultraviolet radiation threatens life, and the time to react is very short.
The story follows a boy who can understand animal language as he works with scientists to find a solution.
Framed as disaster, the film puts environmental awareness and reflection on the relation between humans and nature at its centre.
For Chinese science fiction of the early 1990s, both the subject and the treatment were unusual.
