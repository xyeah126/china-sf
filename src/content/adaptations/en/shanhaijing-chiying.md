A television series directed by Zhu Ruibin, broadcast in 2016 and starring Zhang Han and Guli Nazha.
It builds a secondary world from the beasts and geography of the Classic of Mountains and Seas.
Its adventure narrative turns on the lore of the Azure Dragon, White Tiger and other constellations.
It recasts ancient myth as a youth idol drama.
The adaptation prompted debate about how far a classic text should be turned into entertainment.
