Directed by Stephen Chow and released in 2013, starring Wen Zhang, Shu Qi and Huang Bo.
It retells the origins of the pilgrimage through the young demon-hunter Chen Xuanzang.
Sun Wukong appears as a violent figure whose demonic nature is far from spent, unlike most adaptations.
Dark register and comic rhythm are held together to strong box-office effect.
It brings the novel's demon-subduing dimension to the foreground and opens another reading.
