An animated feature directed by Tian Xiaopeng, released in 2015.
It follows Sun Wukong, pinned beneath Five Elements Mountain, as he meets the boy Jiang Liuer and finds himself again.
Its production values and emotional core won it enthusiastic word of mouth.
It earned close to one billion yuan, and is seen as the turning point in the revival of Chinese animation.
The film proved that traditional myth still carries great force in the animated medium.
