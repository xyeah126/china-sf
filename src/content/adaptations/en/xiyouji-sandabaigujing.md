Directed by Cheang Pou-soi and released in 2016, starring Aaron Kwok, Gong Li and Feng Shaofeng.
It takes up the 'three strikes against the White Bone Demon' episode and centres on the crisis of trust between master and disciple.
Gong Li's White Bone Demon is given a more complicated motive and design.
Its effects and art direction are the strongest in the series.
It is the better received of the films, with a more focused narrative.
