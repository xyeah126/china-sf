Produced and streamed by Bilibili, released in 2022.
It was the first complete screen adaptation of the novel.
Key sequences such as Red Coast and the Three-Body game are rendered in 3D animation.
Reception was divided, and debate about fidelity continued well after broadcast.
It remains an important attempt to industrialise Chinese science fiction animation.
