Directed by Jeffrey Lau and released in 1995, starring Stephen Chow, Karen Mok and Ng Man-tat.
The Moonlight Box prop opens time travel and recasts the prehistory of the pilgrimage.
The romantic thread linking Joker to Baijingjing and Zixia turns myth into a fable of love.
It performed modestly on release but was reclaimed by university and online audiences years later.
With its sequel it became one of the most notable cultural phenomena in Chinese-language cinema.
