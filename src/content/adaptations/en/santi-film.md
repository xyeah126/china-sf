A film project from Enlight Media, announced years ago.
Progress has been repeatedly delayed and the creative team has changed several times.
It is among the most watched and most troubled cases in the screen history of Chinese SF.
Its central difficulty is how to render the vast premises of the Trisolaran world.
It remains listed as in development, with no release date.
