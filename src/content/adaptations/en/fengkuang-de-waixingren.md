Directed by Ning Hao and released in 2019, drawing on Liu Cixin's The Rural Teacher.
Starring Huang Bo and Shen Teng, with Xu Zheng in a special appearance.
It reframes the classic SF motif of first contact as absurdist comedy.
Science fiction premises are transplanted into Chinese street life to sharp comic effect.
It was the first film adaptation of a Liu Cixin-derived work to reach the big screen.
