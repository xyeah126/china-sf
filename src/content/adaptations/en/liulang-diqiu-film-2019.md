Directed by Guo Fan and released in 2019, adapted from Liu Cixin's story of the same name.
Starring Wu Jing, Qu Chuxiao, Li Guangjie and Wu Mengda.
It tells of humanity fitting engines to the Earth to flee the solar crisis with its home in tow.
Its heavy-industrial aesthetic opened the era of Chinese science fiction cinema.
It ranks among China's highest-grossing films and led to the greenlighting of sequels.
