Adapted from Tong Enzheng's short story of the same name, directed by Zhang Hongmei and released in 1980.
It tells of scientists developing a high-efficiency laser weapon and foiling a foreign conspiracy.
It is generally regarded as China's first science fiction feature film.
Under the technical conditions of the day, its science fiction was realised through locations and models.
Its release helped popularise SF literature in the early 1980s.
