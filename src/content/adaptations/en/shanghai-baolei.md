Shanghai Fortress, directed by Teng Huatao and released in 2019, is adapted from Jiang Nan's novel of the same name.
Lu Han plays Jiang Yang and Shu Qi plays Lin Lan; the story is set as an alien civilisation attacks Earth and Shanghai becomes the last line of defence.
The film concentrates on the interplay of war spectacle and personal feeling.
Its reception and ratings were very poor, provoking wide debate about casting, adaptation method and the making of science fiction film in China.
It is seen as a significant setback in the Chinese SF film boom, and often cited in discussions of the difficulty of bringing SF to screen.
