The Moon Man, directed by Zhang Chiyu and released in 2022, is a science fiction comedy adapted from the webtoon of the same name by the Korean artist Cho Seok.
Shen Teng plays Dugu Yue and Ma Li plays Ma Lanxing; the story is set after the Lunar Shield Project, mounted to deflect an asteroid.
A maintenance worker, Dugu Yue, is accidentally left behind on the moon as the last human beyond Earth, and his solitary survival is broadcast to the whole planet.
A comic shell around an SF premise, playing the predicament of an individual under an apocalypse lightly and warmly.
It is a rare Chinese science fiction film led by comedy, and a considerable box-office success.
