The Mermaid, directed by Stephen Chow and released in 2016, stars Deng Chao and Lin Yun.
A land-reclamation project threatens a community of merpeople in a bay, and conflict follows.
The SF and fantasy elements serve an environmental theme: the merpeople are nature as it is squeezed by modern development.
The film continues Chow's tragicomic manner, holding a fairly explicit ecological position beneath its nonsense.
It is among the most successful couplings of environmental science fiction with commercial comedy in Chinese-language cinema, and a strong box-office performer.
