The second half of the Chinese Odyssey diptych, directed by Jeffrey Lau and released in 1995.
Stephen Chow plays Joker, Athena Chu plays Zixia and Karen Mok plays Baijingjing.
Displaced time and missed love lie at its centre, and its city-wall finale is widely quoted.
Its lines about a love once placed before him have become classics of Chinese cinema.
Together with the first film it forms one of the most influential deconstructions of the novel.
