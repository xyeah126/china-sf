Produced by Tencent Video and directed by Yang Lei, broadcast in 2023.
Zhang Luyi plays Wang Miao, Yu Hewei plays Shi Qiang and Chen Jin plays Ye Wenjie.
It won broad approval for a narrative pace and casting close to the novel.
Working with a limited effects budget, it handled SF sequences through realist means.
The series is seen as a milestone in the screen adaptation of Chinese science fiction.
