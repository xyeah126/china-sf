Kungfu Cyborg, directed by Jeffrey Lau and released in 2009, stars Sun Li and Alex Fong.
A robot policeman is posted to a small town, setting off a series of misunderstandings and gags.
The film joins the robot subject to romantic comedy and asks the familiar question whether a machine can understand human feeling.
Its style continues Lau's nonsense comedy; the SF premise serves the comedy above all.
In the line of Chinese science fiction cinema it belongs to the commercially driven comic type.
